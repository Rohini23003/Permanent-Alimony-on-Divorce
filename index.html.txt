    <section>
        <h2>Executive Summary & Trend Analysis</h2>
        <p>This empirical study analyzes 21 reasoned judgments delivered by the Supreme Court of India between September 2021 and August 2026 concerning permanent alimony upon divorce. Key doctrinal shifts highlight the routine invocation of <strong>Article 142 of the Constitution</strong> to grant direct decrees of divorce on grounds of "irretrievable breakdown of marriage," coupled with substantial upward revisions of permanent alimony to ensure parity in living standards.</p>
    </section>
